    Building

    Pull a panel edge outward to add panels in half steps. Pull inward to remove them. Pull around a corner to change plane. With triangle panels enabled in Setup, pull an edge diagonally, toward one end of the edge, to add a triangle panel there: the far corner on the side you pull toward stays and the other is cut. Grab an existing panel's corner and pull it inward along the diagonal to cut it off; pull a cut corner outward to restore the square. Where several panels meet, the pull direction picks the panel. In Select mode, drag across panels to select them, then pick a colour or finish to restyle them. Products and displays: drag a card from Stock your booth onto a shelf or the table (banner stands and gridwall go on the floor behind it, lights clip to panels), then drag items on the display to move them. Tick Lock products to build through them without grabbing them.
